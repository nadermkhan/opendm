Data Transfer Consent

To download files or multimedia content, your browser makes requests to the respective servers over the network. This add-on transfers data of such requests to the OpenDM desktop application to accelerate downloads in OpenDM instead of the browser.

Do you allow OpenDM Integration Module to transfer this data to your local OpenDM application?

   

∗∗∗ This data is stored by OpenDM on your local computer only until you delete the download from OpenDM's list of downloads. OpenDM has zero telemetry.

Full details are provided in our Privacy Policy.

Privacy Policy (Zero Telemetry)

OpenDM Integration Module does not collect or transmit any telemetry, analytics, or user data to any external server. It only forwards download URLs and request headers locally to the OpenDM desktop application running on your machine.

Thank you! You can start using OpenDM for browser downloads now.

Without transferring download URLs to the local OpenDM application, the extension cannot intercept downloads.
